About Us

Made With Tradition

Founded in 1960, our family-owned restaurant believes the key to the heart is through the stomach. Here we value tradition. Our kitchen uses fresh ingredients to create traditional Italian meals.

From handmade pasta to dessert, every dish is prepared thoughtfully and served with Italian love.
